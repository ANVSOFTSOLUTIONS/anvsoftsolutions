FAQ

Common questions.

A few things clients often ask before starting a construction design project with us.

Do you only provide drawings, or do you also build the house?

Both. ANV Constructions handles full construction and contracting for duplexes, villas, and independent homes, as well as AutoCAD design if you only need drawings.

Do you build duplexes and villas?

Yes. We construct duplexes, villas, and small independent homes end-to-end, from foundation to finishing, in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam.

What building drawings can you prepare?

We prepare floor plans, elevations, sections, structural diagrams, site layout plans, and 2D/3D AutoCAD drafting for residential and commercial buildings.

Which cities do you build in?

Our construction and contracting team currently builds in Hyderabad, Vijayawada, and Visakhapatnam. AutoCAD design work is available for clients anywhere.

What software do you use for building diagrams?

Our drafting team uses AutoCAD to produce precise, to-scale floor plans, elevations, and structural drawings ready for approval or construction.
